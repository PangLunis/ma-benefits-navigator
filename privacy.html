Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026 · Benefighter is operated by Parser LLC, a Massachusetts limited liability company · Contact: hello@benefighter.com.

🔒 The free check never sends us your answers. Every answer you type stays in your own web browser on your own device. We never receive it. You can use the whole site anonymously.

Anonymous usage counts: so we can see whether people are able to finish the check, the site sends a tiny anonymous note when a check is started, finished, or left partway — including only which question number was reached, how many questions were shown, and whether it was a phone or a computer. It contains no answers, names, towns, or contact details, uses no cookies, and we do not store IP addresses. It uses a random code that changes on every visit, so visits can't be linked together. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, nothing is sent at all.

Pre-filled forms and worksheets: forms are filled in by your own browser, on your device. The blank form is downloaded from our site, but your answers — and any name, address, birth date, phone number, Medicare number, income or account details you type in — are never sent to us.

Saving your progress: so you can stop and come back, the check can remember your answers and the details you typed for the forms in your own browser on this device (not on our servers). You can turn this off on the first screen of the check, and the results page has a "Forget my answers" button. On a shared or public computer, turn it off.

1. What we collect

Through the website itself: only the anonymous usage counts described above. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply to you.

2. Who else is involved

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and the anonymous usage counts are received by Cloudflare. Like any website host, they may receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) when your browser loads the site. We do not sell or share any personal information.

3. Your choices

You can use the check without saving anything, clear saved answers with the "Forget my answers" button or your browser's settings, and turn on "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" to stop the anonymous counts. To ask us about anything we may hold (such as an email you sent), contact hello@benefighter.com.

4. Security and breach notification

Because the check keeps your answers on your own device, we do not hold them. If a security breach ever involved personal information we hold, we would notify you and the appropriate Massachusetts authorities as required by M.G.L. c.93H.

5. Not a covered entity

We are not a HIPAA covered entity. Although the check asks about Medicare or disability status to identify benefits, we are not your healthcare provider or insurer.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
