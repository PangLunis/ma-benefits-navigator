Common questions

Questions families ask us

Isn't this stuff free? Can't I just do it myself?

Yes — every one of these is a public program you can apply for yourself at no cost, and the check is free too. It finds everything that fits, fills in the state forms from your answers, and tells you exactly where to send them. Free local help is also available from your town's Council on Aging and SHINE counselors.

What does the registered nurse on the team do?

Our co-founder is a registered nurse who works with us as a benefits navigator. Years of working with older adults and their families help us spot the programs people miss and explain them plainly. To be clear: this is benefits navigation, not health care. Nothing we provide is medical advice, a diagnosis, or treatment, and no clinician-patient relationship is created. For medical questions, always talk to your own doctor or provider.

Do you handle VA benefits and taxes?

The check points you to them, but we don't prepare VA claims or file tax returns. VA claims go through a free, VA-accredited Veterans Service Officer, and the Senior Circuit Breaker is claimed on your state tax return — the check gives you a worksheet with your numbers, and free help is available from AARP Foundation Tax-Aide.

My parent doesn't live with me, or is in another town. Is that a problem?

Not at all — many people use the check for a parent. Enter your parent's details and town, and the results, local offices and pre-filled forms will all be for them.

Are you a law firm or a government agency?

No to both. Benefighter is an independent information and document-research service. We are not a government agency, and being contacted by us is not an official notice about your benefits. We are not a law firm — we provide factual information and clerical help, not legal, tax, financial, or medical advice. For legal questions (especially nursing-home MassHealth or asset protection), we'll refer you to a licensed Massachusetts elder-law attorney.

What happens to my personal information?

The free benefits check runs entirely in your browser — your answers are never sent to us. The forms are filled in on your device too. We don't sell anything and we don't track you for marketing. See our privacy policy.
